## DIFFERENTIATION STRATEGY FOR SCRAPEAPI

### Positioning: "The Developer-First Scraping API"

**Key differentiators to pursue:**
1. **Simpler API** — One endpoint, auto-detection of data structure
2. **Transparent pricing** — Per-request, no credit multipliers
3. **Generous free tier** — 100 requests/day (vs trial-only competitors)
4. **Better DX** — Interactive docs, playground, SDKs in 7+ languages
5. **Faster** — Optimized pipeline, <500ms for simple pages
6. **AI extraction** — Natural language selectors ("extract all prices")

**Pricing to undercut:**
| Tier | ScrapeAPI | Competitor Avg | Advantage |
|------|-----------|----------------|-----------|
| Free | 100/day | Trial only | Generous |
| Starter | $19/mo | $49/mo | 60% cheaper |
| Pro | $49/mo | $149-299/mo | 70% cheaper |
| Business | $99/mo | $299-475/mo | 70% cheaper |

**Trade-off:** Smaller proxy network initially, focus on datacenter + selective residential.
